An engineer by degree, a designer at heart.

I was raised on the islands, trained as an engineer, and somewhere along the way, design quietly won me over. All three still show up in how I work.

Almost three years of experience spanning AI-powered workflows, client-based web and mobile applications, and e-commerce. I’ve worked closely with founders, product managers, and engineers to translate messy problems into clear, usable products.

Lately, I’ve been learning to build and ship using AI tools, so I can understand how products come together end to end, and move faster from a design concept to something real.
