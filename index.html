hi, I’m Mridula, a product designer who untangles messy products until they feel simple.

An engineer by degree, a designer at heart.

Know more

I grew up with the waves of the Andaman Islands, studied computer science engineering in Kerala, and found my way to design in Bengaluru. The engineer never left: I care about how things get built, not just how they look.

For three years, I’ve designed across AI marketing, B2B SaaS and e-commerce, working closely with founders on agentic workflows, design systems and the everyday flows people rely on.

AI runs through my whole process, from research and ideation to real-copy designs and working prototypes. I’ve shipped AI products and design with AI daily, so I know where it helps and where it needs a human.
