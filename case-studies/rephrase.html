Rephrase turns "ugh, this deadline is impossible" into a message you can actually send at work, and shows you why it works.

01 - Why I built it

Everyone around me was vibe-coding apps

My feed was full of people shipping whole apps in a weekend with AI, mostly games and fun experiments. I wanted to point the same tools at something more useful: a problem I had actually lived through, taken all the way from an idea to a live product people can use at work.

02 - The problem

Nobody teaches you how to talk at work

When I started working, I thought you just talk normally. Then I noticed everyone around me spoke a different language. They said the same things I wanted to say, but in a way that got taken seriously.

So I said yes to everything

Extra work, impossible deadlines, tasks that weren't mine. Not because I agreed, but because I didn't know how to push back without sounding unprofessional.

03 - The solution

From what you mean to what you can send

Rephrase is one screen. You write the message the way you'd say it to a friend, make three quick choices, and get back a chat-length message you can paste into Slack or Teams, with one line on the move it made.

  1. Pick a situationOptional. Each one carries its own guidance, like framing a deadline as a risk.
  2. Sending toManager, peer, senior or client. This changes the shape of the message.
  3. How firmGentle, balanced or firm. This changes how strongly it's said.
  4. TranslateYour draft goes to the AI with all three choices.
  5. The messageReady to copy, with who it's for and how long it is.
  6. Why this worksNames the technique, so over time you need the tool less.

04 - Key decisions

Fewer controls, each one mapped to how people think

  1. A formality slider, Casual to Formal→"Sending to" + "How firm"

    Nobody thinks "70% formal". They think "it's my manager, and I need to hold my ground".

  2. Tips shown before you start→"Why this works" after the rewrite

    An explanation only means something once there's a message to explain.

  3. Version history with arrows→One Retry button

    Every version is another paid API call, and comparing drafts confused more than it helped.

  4. Email-length messages→Chat length only

    These conversations happen on Slack and Teams, not in long emails.

05 - Design & theme

An open folder on a desk, not another AI dashboard

Most AI tools look the same: dark panels, glowing gradients, a sparkle in every corner. I wanted Rephrase to feel like sitting down at your desk with a file in front of you: your messy draft on the left page, the clean version on the right, and the clutter of a working day around it.

07 - The logic

One message, four ways

Who you're writing to changes the shape of the message.

The draft

this deadline is impossible. we're all burnt out and nobody asked us before committing to friday

Manager

Flagging a risk on Friday's deadline. At this pace we won't hit it without cutting quality, and the team's already stretched. Could we talk today about moving the date or trimming scope?

Peer

Honestly, Friday isn't doable without cutting corners. Want to push back on it together before planning?

Senior

Friday's deadline is at risk; we'll need a new date or a smaller scope to deliver it well. Happy to share options today.

Client

Hi, we want this release to meet the standard you expect, so we'd like to propose a revised date. Could we find 15 minutes this week to agree on the plan?

That’s all, folks! :)

For years, my ideas stopped at the prototype. A clickable Figma file was as far as they got. With Rephrase, I typed a messy thought into a box I'd designed, pressed a button, and watched it come back as something I'd actually send.

AI didn't just make this faster. It let me cross the line from designing a product to building one. Seeing something I imagined live on the internet, working, felt amazing, and I'm not going back to stopping at the prototype.
