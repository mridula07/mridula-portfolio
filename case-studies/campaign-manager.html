Helping Solopreneurs Run Campaigns Without Operational Chaos

Designing an AI campaign manager that helps solopreneurs maintain momentum without operational chaos.

Role
Product Designer
Industry
AI Marketing / SaaS
Platform
Desktop Web
Core focus
Agentic UX, Workflow Design
Duration
12 Weeks

01 - overview

What Is This Project?

An AI-powered campaign management system designed for solopreneurs who need to run multi-platform campaigns without a marketing team. The central intelligence is Rex, AI agent that captures intent, structures campaigns, and run them.

More than just a content generation, copywriting, scheduling, or publishing tool, it helps users plan and run campaigns through a structured workflow that keeps everything organised in one place.

02 - The problem

The campaign lifecycle breaks at every hand-off

Solopreneurs usually have the idea. The friction starts when planning, drafting, scheduling, and follow-through happen across disconnected tools.

  1. Campaign IdeaIdea stays in a notes app
  2. PlanningPlan gets retyped into prompts
  3. Content CreationDrafts copied into a scheduler
  4. SchedulingPosted platform by platform
  5. PublishingResults spread across dashboards
  6. Follow-through

  1. 01Too many tools

    Planning, content creation, scheduling, publishing, and tracking are typically handled across multiple tools. As campaigns grow, keeping everything organised requires additional effort and constant context switching.

  2. 02Difficult for Non-Marketers

    Campaign tools often assume familiarity with audience targeting, content strategy, platform selection, and campaign planning. For solopreneurs without a marketing background, even getting started can feel complicated.

  3. 03Hard to Manage

    Once a campaign is underway, content, schedules, platform activity, and launch requirements all need to stay aligned. Without a structured system, coordination becomes increasingly difficult as more moving parts are introduced.

03 - goals

The solution needed to reduce complexity without taking control away from users.

  1. 01

    Make Campaign Creation More Accessible

    Campaigns should be easy to create and manage without requiring deep marketing knowledge or prior experience.

  2. 02

    Bring The Workflow Together

    Planning, content creation, scheduling, publishing, and tracking should feel like one connected experience instead of separate activities spread across multiple tools.

  3. 03

    Simplify Campaign Management

    Users should be able to understand what has been planned, what is ready, and what still needs attention without manually coordinating every part of the campaign.

04 - core insight

The real gap wasn’t content creation. It was turning ideas into campaigns.

Most tools help generate content, write copy, schedule posts, or publish across platforms. The larger challenge lies in planning, coordinating, and executing a campaign from start to finish.

The opportunity was not to create another content tool, but to build a system that could turn campaign intent into coordinated execution.

05 - Key product decisions

What Changed And Why

01One Question at a Time → Grouped Decision Cards

Early exploration

Users answered campaign questions one by one through a sequential conversation.

Issue

Reduce panic and help users recover quickly during an unexpected disruption.

Final decision

Related inputs were grouped into structured decision cards.

Outcome

Reduced interaction steps and made campaign setup easier to scan and update.

02Chat Composer → In-Context Selection

Early exploration

Users responded through a traditional chat input at the bottom of the screen.

Issue

Most responses were structured selections rather than free-form conversations.

Final decision

Selections were embedded directly within Rex’s messages.

Outcome

The experience became more guided and eliminated unnecessary interaction.

03Immediate Generation → Strategy Selection

Early exploration

Campaigns were generated immediately after collecting inputs.

Issue

Users had little visibility into why the campaign was structured a certain way.

Final decision

Rex presents multiple campaign approaches before generation.

Outcome

Users remain in control and understand the trade-offs behind each direction.

06 - Final design

Walking through the happy flow, screen by screen.

Solopreneurs usually have the idea. The friction starts when planning, drafting, scheduling, and follow-through happen across disconnected tools.

01Campaign Intent

Every campaign starts with a few key decisions. Rex captures the campaign objective, platforms, duration, and primary goal upfront to establish the right context before moving into planning.

Why this approach?

  • Captures the campaign intent early
  • Keeps related decisions together
  • Creates a strong foundation for the steps that follow

02Campaign Refinement

Rex uses this step to understand who the content is for, when the campaign starts, and how often it should run. These inputs help shape the content direction and make the later strategy recommendations more relevant.

Why this approach?

  • Helps Rex tailor content to the right audience
  • Adds context before strategy generation
  • Keeps the campaign setup focused and easy to review

03Campaign Analysis

Rather than showing a generic loading state, Rex surfaces what it’s currently working on. This gives users visibility into the planning process and reassures them that the campaign is actively being structured before recommendations are generated.

System Status

  • Reviewing campaign
  • Mapping campaign pace
  • Building post order
  • Evaluating rollout
  • Balancing platforms

04Strategy Selection

There is no single way to run a successful campaign. Instead of generating content immediately, Rex presents multiple rollout approaches based on the campaign objective, audience, and timeline. This allows users to compare strategies, understand the rationale behind each one, and choose the direction that best fits their goals.

Why this step matters

  • Gives users control over the campaign direction
  • Makes strategy decisions easier for non-marketers
  • Creates transparency before content generation begins

05Campaign Workspace

Once a strategy is selected, Rex transforms it into a campaign workspace where planning and execution come together. Instead of leaving users with a strategy document or a list of generated posts, the campaign is organised into a timeline that shows what is planned, what is ready, and what still needs attention before launch.

Why this step matters

  • Turns strategy into an actionable campaign plan
  • Gives visibility into campaign progress and readiness
  • Brings content, scheduling, and platform setup into one place

06Reviewing & Editing Content

Before launch, users can review every post individually, inspect the generated assets, and make adjustments where needed. Opening a post reveals the content, scheduling details, platform assignment, and generation inputs that were used to create it.

Why this step matters

  • Gives users control before publishing
  • Makes AI-generated content easy to review and refine
  • Keeps edits connected to the overall campaign plan

07 - Digging Deeper

The Details That Shape The Experience

0.1Editing Previous Decisions

Campaign decisions remain editable throughout. If a user changes an earlier input, the workflow updates the dependent outputs instead of forcing a restart. This keeps the campaign flexible while still making the impact of each change visible.

Key behaviour

Changing one decision visibly updates the outputs that depend on it.

0.2Campaign Completion & Visibility

A campaign can only move forward when every required component is in place. Rex highlights missing platform connections, recipient groups, and setup requirements early, helping users resolve issues before they impact the launch.

Key behaviour

Launch blockers are identified before they become launch failures.

0.3Adding WhatsApp Recipients

WhatsApp requires a different setup from the organic platforms. Instead of connecting a publishing account alone, the workflow also needs a recipient group before the campaign can go live. This keeps the distribution logic aligned with how WhatsApp actually works.

Key behaviour

WhatsApp behaves like a direct distribution channel, not just another social platform.

0.4Campaign And Post States

The product uses simple states to make progress easy to understand. Campaigns move through a small lifecycle, while individual posts show their publishing status inside the workspace. This gives users a quick sense of what is still being prepared, what is ready, and what has already gone live.

Key behaviour

Status stays clear and lightweight — never overloaded with labels.

08 - Key Learnings

Lessons beyond the interface

  1. 01

    Every Decision Has a Trade-off

    There is rarely a perfect solution. Designing means balancing speed and depth, guidance and flexibility, automation and control, while understanding what matters most in a given context.

  2. 02

    Great Experiences Are Built Across Systems

    The most meaningful improvements often come from connecting workflows, information, and decisions together. Individual screens matter, but the experience between them matters more.

  3. 03

    Trust Must Be Designed

    People are more likely to adopt a product when they understand what is happening and why. Transparency, feedback, and visibility can be just as important as functionality.

  4. 04

    Progress Beats Perfection

    Waiting for the perfect solution can slow down learning. Shipping, testing, and refining ideas often leads to better outcomes than trying to solve everything upfront.
